Elephant toothpaste

Time: 25 minutes · FRC 6429 Sense of Science

Materials

  • Empty bottle
  • 3% hydrogen peroxide (from a pharmacy)
  • Dry yeast
  • Warm water
  • Dish soap, colouring
  • Tray

Method

  1. Place the bottle on the tray. Put in half a cup of hydrogen peroxide, a little dish soap and colouring.
  2. In a separate container, mix 1 tablespoon of yeast with 3 tablespoons of warm water.
  3. Pour the yeast mixture into the bottle and step back.
  4. Watch the foam overflow from the bottle.

Why did it happen?

Yeast quickly breaks hydrogen peroxide into water and oxygen. The oxygen makes foam bubbles with the soap. This is called a catalyst effect.

Think and try

  • Is the foam warm? Does this reaction give off heat?
  • What happens if you try it without yeast?

Safety

Use only 3% hydrogen peroxide, do it with an adult and wear goggles. Do not touch the foam for long without gloves. Do the experiment with an adult.
