Lava lamp

Time: 20 minutes · FRC 6429 Sense of Science

Materials

  • Clear bottle
  • Vegetable oil
  • Water
  • Food colouring
  • Effervescent (fizzy) vitamin tablet

Method

  1. Fill three quarters of the bottle with oil.
  2. Add the water on top and see that the oil stays above.
  3. Add a few drops of colouring; it sinks into the water.
  4. Break the tablet into pieces and drop them in.
  5. Watch the moving coloured bubbles.

Why did it happen?

Oil is lighter than water and does not mix. The tablet makes gas in water; the gas bubbles carry coloured water up, and when the gas escapes the water sinks again.

Think and try

  • Why does the oil stay on top of the water?
  • What happens if you add more tablets?

Safety

Do not swallow the tablet. Do not close the bottle, gas builds pressure. Do the experiment with an adult.
