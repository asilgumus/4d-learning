Dancing pepper

Time: 10 minutes · FRC 6429 Sense of Science

Materials

  • Plate
  • Water
  • Black pepper
  • Dish soap
  • Finger

Method

  1. Fill the plate with water and sprinkle black pepper on the surface.
  2. Dip your dry finger into the water. What happened to the pepper?
  3. Dip your finger in dish soap, then into the water.
  4. Watch the pepper rush to the edges.

Why did it happen?

The water surface behaves like a light 'skin' (surface tension). Soap lowers this tension and the pepper is pulled and pushed to the edges.

Think and try

  • What happens if you try it with other liquids (oil, milk)?
  • What happens when the soap on your finger runs out?

Safety

Wash your hands before touching your eyes. Do the experiment with an adult.
