Gas that inflates a balloon

Time: 15 minutes · FRC 6429 Sense of Science

Materials

  • Empty plastic bottle
  • Balloon
  • Vinegar
  • Baking soda (bicarbonate)
  • Funnel

Method

  1. Put half a cup of vinegar in the bottle.
  2. Use the funnel to put 2 teaspoons of baking soda into the balloon.
  3. Stretch the neck of the balloon over the mouth of the bottle.
  4. Lift the balloon, pour the baking soda into the bottle and watch.

Why did it happen?

Vinegar (an acid) and baking soda (a base) react and release carbon dioxide gas. When the gas has no room in the bottle, it fills the balloon.

Think and try

  • Does more baking soda make a bigger balloon?
  • Why did the balloon inflate, can you hear anything?

Safety

Wear goggles and do not seal the bottle with its cap. Do the experiment with an adult.
