Floating egg

Time: 15 minutes · FRC 6429 Sense of Science

Materials

  • Egg
  • Tall glass
  • Water
  • Salt (6–8 tablespoons)
  • Spoon

Method

  1. Fill the glass halfway with water and gently drop in the egg: it sinks to the bottom.
  2. Take the egg out. Add the salt to the water, stirring with the spoon.
  3. Put the egg back: does it float?
  4. Slowly add plain water on top and watch how the egg moves.

Why did it happen?

Salt increases the density of water. When the water is denser than the egg, the egg is pushed up and floats. This is also why people don't sink in the Dead Sea.

Think and try

  • How many spoons of salt did it take for the egg to float?
  • Could you make the egg sink and float again?

Safety

Wash your hands after touching a raw egg. Do the experiment with an adult.
