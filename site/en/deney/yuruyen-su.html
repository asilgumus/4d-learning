Walking water

Time: 20 minutes · FRC 6429 Sense of Science

Materials

  • 6 plastic cups
  • Paper towel
  • Red, yellow and blue food colouring
  • Water

Method

  1. Line up the cups side by side and fill every other one (1st, 3rd, 5th) halfway with water.
  2. Add red to the 1st cup, yellow to the 3rd and blue to the 5th.
  3. Cut the paper towel into 6 strips and fold them in half.
  4. Dip one end of each strip into a full cup and the other end into an empty cup.
  5. Wait a few minutes and watch the colours mix in the empty cups.

Why did it happen?

The tiny gaps between the fibres of the paper towel pull water upward. This is called capillary action. Plants carry water from their roots to their leaves in the same way.

Think and try

  • Which colours mixed and what colour did they make?
  • Does a thick towel or a thin one pull faster?

Safety

Food colouring stains clothes, so wear an apron. Do the experiment with an adult.
