Make your own compass

Time: 20 minutes · FRC 6429 Sense of Science

Materials

  • Sewing needle
  • Magnet
  • Cork or a small piece of foam
  • Bowl full of water

Method

  1. Rub the needle against the magnet 30–40 times in the same direction.
  2. Place the needle horizontally on the piece of cork.
  3. Put the cork on the bowl of water.
  4. Watch the needle turn by itself and settle in one direction, and compare it with a real compass.

Why did it happen?

The Earth is like a giant magnet. The magnetized needle follows this magnetic field and settles in a north–south direction.

Think and try

  • Which direction does the needle point?
  • What happens if you bring another magnet close?

Safety

The needle is sharp. An adult must help. Do the experiment with an adult.
