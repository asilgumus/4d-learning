Magic milk

Time: 15 minutes · FRC 6429 Sense of Science

Materials

  • Whole milk
  • Wide plate
  • Food colourings in different colours
  • Dish soap
  • Cotton swab

Method

  1. Pour the milk onto the plate.
  2. Drop the colourings randomly onto the milk.
  3. Dip the tip of the cotton swab in dish soap.
  4. Lightly touch the middle of the milk with the swab and watch the colours dance.

Why did it happen?

The soap pushes away the fats and proteins in the milk and lowers the surface tension. The colours rush off in all directions because of this movement.

Think and try

  • Does it work with skim milk too?
  • What else could you try instead of soap, and what happens?

Safety

Do not drink the milk and do not put your mouth on the soap. Do the experiment with an adult.
