Elevator

Manufacture4Youth guide · Primary and middle school, with an adult

A lift model that raises a load with a pulley system. The booklet goes step by step from the base to the roof.

Preview (3D)

Preparing the 3D model…
Drag: rotate · Wheel: zoom

Steps at a glance

  1. Place the base piece.
  2. Attach the two horizontal pieces upright.
  3. Place the small base piece so that the holes line up.
  4. Attach the small horizontal pieces with the flat side facing you.
  5. Attach the back piece.
  6. Attach the top piece.
  7. Attach the roof.
  8. Attach the parts that the pulley will be mounted on.
