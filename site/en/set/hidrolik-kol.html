Hydraulic Arm

Manufacture4Youth guide · Primary and middle school, with an adult

A hydraulic robot arm that works with syringes and tubes. When you push one syringe, the fluid moves to the other and the arm moves.

Preview (3D)

Preparing the 3D model…
Drag: rotate · Wheel: zoom

Steps at a glance

  1. Place the base piece.
  2. Attach the first piece upright and add the supports next to it.
  3. Attach the second piece upright; place the rod and the syringe in the middle.
  4. Attach the upper arm and seat the syringe.
  5. Attach the tubes of the syringes.
  6. Attach the Arduino and the wheels, and connect them.
  7. Attach the other wheels, insert the battery and connect it.
