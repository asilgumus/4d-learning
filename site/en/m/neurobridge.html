Guide for instructors and families

Before you start

Don't force the game on the child. Play one level together, then let the child decide. A quiet room and enough light are enough.

Levels

The 15 levels teach direction commands, repetition (loops), jumping and sequential thinking through single-command movement.

When they get stuck

Don't give the solution. Ask 'What should we do first? Then?' Ask the child to say their own steps out loud.

Adjust the pace

Take a break at signs of tiredness. 10–15 minutes per session is enough. Celebrate small successes.

Accessibility

The minimal interface was designed to reduce distractions. You can reach us on GitHub for feedback and suggestions.
