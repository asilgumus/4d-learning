Cybersecurity

A 17-slide presentation (in Turkish): password security, social media and privacy, dangerous links, phishing, Trojans, spyware and man-in-the-middle attacks.

Primary schoolMiddle school40–50 min

Presentation

Download PDF (Turkish)

Discussion questions

Password

What does your password consist of? Could someone guess it?

Link

What do you do first when someone you don't know sends you a link? Who do you show it to?

Wi-Fi

Why is it risky to log in to a banking app on a café's open Wi-Fi?

Bullying

If someone online treats you badly, who do you tell and what evidence do you keep?

Presenter notes

Time

15 min presentation, 15 min discussion, 10 min password game.

Password game

Each child writes their own password rule (not their real password) on paper; the class votes whether it is strong or weak.

Caution

Do not ask children for real passwords or personal information.
